          privacy.audit

          Built like a security tool

          A focus app sees what you do on your phone, so the first rule is to keep that data with you.

          • Privacy first

            All usage data stays on this device. There is no account, no analytics and nothing is uploaded or sold.

          • Minimal permissions

            This website asks for none. The Android app will ask only for what blocking needs and explain each one before you agree.

          • Tamper protection

            A PIN is needed to end a session early, turn off Night Mode or change the bedtime, so a weak moment cannot undo your plan. Three wrong tries lock the PIN for 30 seconds.

          • Digital wellbeing awareness

            Short tips below explain how apps are designed to hook you, so you can spot the trick.

            threat.model
            
      
            How apps are built to hook you
            
      
            Know the trick and it loses some of its pull.
            
    
            
    
              
      
            • Infinite scroll
              A feed with no bottom has no natural moment to stop. Your brain never gets the cue that it is finished.
              Counter: decide your stopping point before you open the app.
              • 
      
            • Notification tricks
              Red badges and pings are designed to feel urgent. Almost none of them are.
              Counter: mute everything for the next 25 minutes.
              • 
      
            • Pull to refresh
              Sometimes nothing new appears, sometimes something great does. That uncertainty works like a slot machine.
              Counter: check at set times, not on impulse.
              • 
      
            • Autoplay
              The next video starts before you decide anything. If you do not choose, the app chooses for you.
              Counter: turn autoplay off in settings.
